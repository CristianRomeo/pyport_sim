Method

Savings are what solar and battery add on top of smart charging: the weekly grid cost with the optimizer and no plant, minus the weekly cost with the plant. Four representative weeks (January, April, July, October 2025) stand for a full year. Without a grid cap every scenario serves all planned trips. In a capped case (80, 120 or 150 kW) trips the grid limit does not allow are lost: the plant recovers some of them, and the Service slider puts a value on each recovered trip. Battery power above a quarter of its kWh is charged separately. Smart charging alone is reported separately because it costs nothing to adopt.

NPV, payback and battery replacements are computed as in the thesis code (economics/payback.py). Only the simulated cases are available, because the savings of a new plant size need a new simulation run.
