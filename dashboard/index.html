Method

Savings are what solar and battery add on top of smart charging: the weekly grid cost with the optimizer and no plant, minus the weekly cost with the plant. Four representative weeks (January, April, July, October 2025) stand for a full year, and every scenario serves all planned trips. Smart charging alone is reported separately because it costs nothing to adopt.

Solar energy that the marina does not use is sold to the grid at the export price. The simulations assumed 0.05 €/kWh and the optimizer knew it. Changing the price here re-prices the energy that the simulated plant exported, without running the optimizer again, so it is exact only near the simulated price. Reference points: a Portuguese supplier quotes about 0.05 €/kWh fixed for surplus, and the Madeira UPAC instructions pay 90 % of the monthly OMIE average.

NPV, payback and battery replacements are computed as in the thesis code (economics/payback.py). Only the simulated cases are available, because the savings of a new plant size need a new simulation run.
